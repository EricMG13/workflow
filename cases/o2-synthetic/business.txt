NORTHBRIDGE SYNTHETIC GROUP
Business and ownership note
Published January 10, 2026.
This invented case is test data, not evidence about a real issuer.
Northbridge Synthetic Group is an industrial products manufacturer. It designs, manufactures and sells fastening and sealing components for industrial equipment.
Customers are industrial distributors that serve maintenance, repair and operations demand, and original-equipment manufacturers supplied under annual supply agreements.
All operations and all reported revenue are in the United States.
Management describes the group as a leading supplier with a resilient aftermarket franchise.
Revenue is reported as one operating segment, Consolidated Operations. The split of revenue between fastening and sealing components, and between distributors and original-equipment manufacturers, is not disclosed.
The share of revenue from the largest customer is not disclosed.
NORTHBRIDGE SYNTHETIC GROUP
Operating model and ownership
List prices are reviewed once a year. Management states that raw-material cost increases are passed to customers with a lag of one to two quarters.
Volumes follow distributor restocking cycles and original-equipment production schedules.
Cost of sales consists mainly of steel, elastomer and manufacturing labour.
Capital expenditure maintains existing plants; no growth capital programme is under way.
Working capital consists of trade receivables, inventories and trade payables.
The issuer is wholly owned by Harbour Ridge Holdings Limited, a synthetic holding company.
Harbour Ridge Holdings Limited is held by private investors. The identity and percentage holdings of those investors are not disclosed.
The issuer paid a dividend to Harbour Ridge Holdings Limited in each quarter of 2025 under a shareholder distribution policy approved by the board.
No dividend recapitalisation, shareholder loan or related-party payment other than these dividends is reported.
The source set identifies no financial sponsor.
NORTHBRIDGE SYNTHETIC GROUP
Financing, transactions and history
The only borrowing of the group is the Northbridge Term Loan, a senior secured term loan with final maturity on December 31, 2029.
No acquisition, disposal, new financing, refinancing or change of ownership occurred in 2025, and none is announced or pending as of January 10, 2026.
This note does not describe the security package, guarantees or covenants of the Northbridge Term Loan.
The business was founded in 2011 as a family-owned manufacturer. Harbour Ridge Holdings Limited acquired the group in 2019.
No credit rating from a rating agency has been assigned.
