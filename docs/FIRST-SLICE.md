IFRS source set, USD millions. Financial notes, quarters, segment and facility
information, guarantor and restricted-group perimeters, and other declared gaps
remain unavailable. This does not qualify PDF/OCR extraction, live provider/model
behavior, all 26 intended owners, all pathways, or production deployment.
