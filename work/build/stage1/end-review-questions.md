- **R3 L1 (quote-side wrap before a minus).** The page prints `-0.25` unwrapped, and the quote is `…is -\n0.25 percent…`, which `quoted` accepts through the joined quote. CP-8 refuses it. Rule W makes the quote itself signed (`- 0.25`), so this may close under W. Please confirm W settles it, or rule that the page test must fail when no needle is found (CP-8's behaviour). Probe: p_quote_wrap_sign.py.
