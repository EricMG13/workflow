# CP-1C — peer benchmark: metric set, thresholds and recorded omissions

Moved from `docs/methods-drafts/cp1c.md` at Stage 2 (`docs/CP1C-OWNER-LEASE.md:7`); the Stage 2 notes at the end are new.

Method basis: `methodology.json` CP-1C "Peer benchmark" ("benchmarks the issuer against a defined peer universe and flags outliers"; required CP-0, CP-1, no optional edge). The r14 CP-1C SKILL.md is not in this repository, so the metrics, thresholds and omissions below are a LEAD-accepted adaptation (rulings 2-4, 6, 7, 15), not a transcription. Execution split (`owner-harness-architecture.md:59`): Python owns normalised peer metrics, medians, positions and outlier flags; the analyst owns the universe and comparability judgments; the rendered section is FP03 `peer-positioning`.

## What the module does

- Four metrics, aligned to CP-1 T4.10 (the LTM Q4 column of the run's base year; LTM Q4 25 when the run has no context): adjusted EBITDA margin (EBITDA / revenue), total leverage (total debt / EBITDA), interest coverage (EBITDA / interest expense), FFO to total debt (FFO / total debt). One division of exact decimals each, 38 digits half-even. The issuer's values are copies of the CP-1 cells.
- Universe: the analyst assesses each peer on the peer summary Comparable, Partly comparable (mandatory limitation) or Not comparable. Comparable and Partly comparable are included; Not comparable is shown, normalised and kept out of every statistic.
- Statistics (over included peers with a calculated value, at least three, otherwise the statistic is missing and never 0): median (odd: middle value; even: (a + b) / 2 on the stored values), issuer minus median (multiples in x; percent metrics in percentage points, unit pp), issuer position (1 + number of entities strictly better, ties share the position, direction per metric) of n + 1 entities, deviation from the median and outlier flag (outlier iff the relative deviation is strictly above one half; exactly one half is Within range).
- Position is descriptive. CP-1C owns it; consumers may read it but never re-rank (ruling 5).
- Derived facts over peers carry the period "Mixed: issuer LTM Q4 YY, each peer's own twelve months", where LTM Q4 YY is the tag of the run's base year (LTM Q4 25 when the run has no context); each peer's own period stays on its source and metric cells.
- No peer source in the pack: the module is limitation-only (issuer cells known, every peer-derived cell missing). The synthetic peer summary is a test vector for cases and fixtures only and is never part of a product pack (ruling 1).

## Recorded omissions (each is a limitation, not a defect)

1. The median includes flagged outliers: an outlier is flagged against the median, not removed from it; the flag is a screen, not a credit judgment.
2. No calendarisation: a peer whose twelve months end on another date is the analyst's Partly comparable with a limitation; Python does not shift or prorate periods (ruling 15). Ratios are currency-neutral, so there is no FX step.
3. No size, rating, sector or business-mix universe criteria. The universe is whatever the data desk supplies, narrowed only by the analyst's comparability judgment.
4. No definition restatement. Peer ratios use the definitions the desk states, including its add-backs as the desk asserts them; the issuer line on the same summary is tied to CP-1 (definition alignment table) but no peer is restated to the issuer's basis.
5. No net leverage, free cash flow, capital expenditure, market-based or valuation metrics in the eight tables. The pack carries no market data; the only valuation figures are the user's own EV and EBITDA entries in the run context, shown in T1C.9 to T1C.11 below, and relative value otherwise belongs to CP-3 (ruling 7).
6. No dispersion statistics (quartiles, range, standard deviation). The reference Fig. RV.3 range strip is drawn by the report from the settled T1C.3, T1C.4 and T1C.5 facts (RV.3a percent, RV.3b multiple) above the peer-positioning tabs; CP-1C adds no fact for it and fills no other chart.
7. Medians over three or four peers are fragile: they move with a single peer. The module states this as a limitation whenever fewer than five peers are included.
8. The minimum of three included peers, the half-median outlier distance and the inclusion rule are policy constants (rulings 2-4), not findings; each is one number in the oracle builder.

## Stage 2 notes

- **One adapter.** Submit, the entry validator, probe and view reach the core only through `cp1c._inputs`: the issuer name from the run, the pack sources in the fixed H1 scope (identity, financial and, when held, peers; `host._OWNER_SCOPES`), the four CP-1 T4.10 facts of the run's LTM Q4 column (LTM Q4 25 when the run has no context) selected by label (`cp1_fact_selection_rule`: exactly one match each, zero or several fail closed as the issue `CP-1 facts unavailable: ...`) and the submission.
- **Parse.** The retained payload is read only through `live.schema.json_object` (protocol section 6); text it refuses is the shape issue `(root): not a JSON object within the payload bounds` and never reaches the schema.
- **Issue lines.** Every line that quotes the payload (the peer name in the unknown-peer, no-peer-source, evidence and limitation lines; a `prose.issues` line, which can quote a payload word whole) is cut at 600 characters by `execution.clipped` before it is built whole, and the list is bounded by `execution.listed_issues`: at most 20 lines, the last counting the rest. A raised composition joins only that bounded list.
- **Model text.** Each rationale becomes `Claim.text`, so after the content rules pass it is also checked as `RunText` through one `TypeAdapter` (rulings stage 2 round 3 item 2); the line is `<path>: <message>` without the input. The gap fields, the limitations and the analyst's peer name are stored as plain text (`Fact.text`, `Claim.limitation`, own limitation lines) and are never built as run text.
- **Blank quotes.** A citation quote that `prose.is_blank` (whitespace, control (`Cc`) and format (`Cf`) characters, the default-ignorable code points that `str.strip()` keeps (U+034F, U+115F, U+1160, U+17B4, U+17B5, U+180B-U+180F, U+3164, U+FE00-U+FE0F, U+FFA0, U+E0100-U+E01EF) and U+2800, or the empty string) is refused as `<path>: empty text` (for example `assessments[2].evidence[0].quote: empty text`) before the page-occurrence check (round 13 addendum 3). Before Stage 2 such a quote was already refused, as a quote/locator mismatch of its empty reading, so a blank quote was never accepted.
- **Source quotes (AGENTS.md "Source quotes").** CP-1C reads no figure from a model quote. Every peer figure comes from the peer table that Python parses from the pack page (the table rule above); the issuer's figures are the copied CP-1 facts. An analyst citation is checked only for occurrence under its located page, plus the blank refusal.
- **Peer table guard limits.** The table rule reads an amount only as ASCII digits with at most one decimal place, or `Not stated`. A grouped amount (`1,250.0`, item 8 comma cost), a decimal comma (`200,0`), two decimal places (`200.00`), a parenthesised negative (`(200.0)`), a unit or currency on the amount (`200.0m`, `US$200.0`) or a figure split by a space (`2 00.0`) is outside the rule, so the whole table is `Peer table not found` (fails closed). A field is split from the next only by two or more spaces. No reading rule is added for any of these.
- **Derived-fact citations.** The lineage rule (lease "lineage citations", `expected.json lineage_rule`; rulings stage2 round 3 item 3, applied at the Stage 2 close release, round 5 item 17): a calculated fact has one `Citation` per (source, page), quoting the first of its inputs' quotes on that page, in `derives_from` order, transitively through calculated facts (`deploy_workflow.owners.blank.one_per_page`; a median cites the first included peer row, not every row). A copied CP-1 cell keeps CP-1's own citations, at that cell's place in `derives_from` order. No quote is invented. The oracle's literal lists were re-pinned to this rule with the change.
- **Synthetic limitation.** On a synthetic case settlement appends "Synthetic case; not real issuer qualification" to the module's own limitations (deduplicated); the oracle lists are the pre-settle lists.
- **Reader (D3 open).** `cp1c_reader(store, head)` returns the head's status and hash, the facts the lease pins for a consumer (`T1C.4.<metric>.median`, `.difference`, `.position`, `.outlier_count` for each metric, then `T1C.8.total.included`) verbatim, and the settled own limitations (with the synthetic line on a synthetic case). A blocked head gives its status and hash only; a stale head or another module's head raises. No consumer reads it yet, so no completion is claimed.
- **View tree.** The key tree of the `read_inputs` view is committed in `cases/cp1c-owner/cp1c-view-tree.json` (shape only, per case), checked by the connect test against the view over a real store and by the owner test against the genuine run's body.

## Valuation tables from the run context (T1C.9 to T1C.11)

Wording only; the arithmetic is `owners/valuation_basis.py` and the composition `owners/cp1c.py` (oracle `cases/valuation-context-owner/expected.json`).

- **When they appear.** Only when the pack holds a run context with a valuation to show. That is: `peer_source` is `user_comps`; or `peer_source` is `user_list` or `cp_dr_public` and the basis is `auto`, `public_comps` or `blend`; or the context lists precedents; or the basis is `auto`, `issuer_trading` or `user_multiple`. Otherwise the head is the eight tables above, unchanged.
- **The tables.** T1C.9 "EV/EBITDA records" has one row per peer or precedent (kind, name, figures date, source, currency, EBITDA period, enterprise value, EBITDA, EV/EBITDA, outlier flag, result). T1C.10 "EV/EBITDA statistics" has one row per set (public comps, precedents): records, admitted, minimum, median, maximum, outliers. For `issuer_trading` and `user_multiple` the row holds the one value as minimum, median and maximum and has no outlier test. T1C.11 "Valuation basis selection" has one row: requested basis, selected basis, reason, EBITDA basis, selected multiple, issuer EBITDA and implied enterprise value.
- **User-supplied records.** The enterprise value, EBITDA, source and date of each peer (`as_of`) and precedent (`date`) are the user's own entries in the context file, cited by line. CP-1C does not verify them against a source; a limitation says so. Peer figures come only from `peer_source: user_comps`.
- **Windows.** A peer's figures must be dated no earlier than 12 months, and a precedent no earlier than 36 months, before the run's `as_of`, counted back by calendar months on the same day (clamped to the month end). A record dated before the window start is excluded, as is a record with no date. The windows apply whatever basis is requested, `public_comps`, `precedents` and `blend` included.
- **Admission.** A record is admitted when its enterprise value and EBITDA are finite and above zero and it is inside its window. Its multiple is EV / EBITDA, exact, 38 digits half-even. An excluded record keeps its row with the reason and stays out of every statistic.
- **Statistics.** The minimum and maximum need one admitted record. The median needs at least three, otherwise it reads "Not calculable: N admitted of 3 required", as do the outlier count and the flags. The outlier flag is the half-median distance rule of the eight tables, over the set median.
- **Selection rules.**
  - `public_comps` and `precedents`: the median of that set's admitted multiples; with fewer than three admitted there is no multiple and no fallback.
  - `blend`: the plain average of the two medians, equal weights; each set needs three admitted records, otherwise no multiple and no fallback.
  - `auto`: the public comps median when that set has three admitted records, else the precedents median when it has three, else the gap "Insufficient Information".
  - `issuer_trading`: the issuer's stated or market-cap-plus-net-debt enterprise value over CP-1's LTM adjusted EBITDA. `user_multiple`: the user's value.
  - The selected multiple is rounded half-even to two decimal places. If it rounds above 99.99 or below 0.01 no multiple is selected (never clamped), because the CP-4C field does not allow it.
  - The implied enterprise value is the selected multiple times CP-1's LTM adjusted EBITDA when that is a known positive amount; an NTM multiple is not applied to LTM EBITDA and has no implied value.
- **Currency and period not stated.** The user states neither the currency nor the EBITDA period of a peer or precedent: both cells read "Not stated by the user", the figures date reads "Date not stated by the user" when absent, and a limitation says each multiple assumes its EV and EBITDA share both and that the selected multiple is applied to the issuer's LTM EBITDA of the run's base year. CP-1C converts nothing.
- **The O7d gap.** `cp_dr_public` peers need the CP-DR peer pack and `user_list` names need a peer lookup (both Part B O7d); CP-1C reads neither. A `user_list` name gets a row of named gaps, and `public_comps` with either source selects no multiple. Under `auto` the precedents are tried, and if they are thin the gap is stated with the peer-source reason.
- **Reader.** A head that holds T1C.11 also gives a consumer `T1C.11.selection.selected_multiple`, `.selected_basis`, `.ebitda_basis` and `.implied_ev` verbatim; CP-4C reads them. A head without T1C.11 gives none.
