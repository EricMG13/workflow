## Why

methodology.json: CP-2G "Builds the forward credit model: deleveraging path and forecast cases". Distressed pathway order CP-2A, CP-2G, CP-2H, CP-4C. CP-2G is the first module that consumes another owner's frozen output, so this lease also closes the open D3 consumption of CP-2A and CP-1D. The architecture study row is "EBITDA/FCF/net debt/leverage path; assumptions, guidance; req CP-0, CP-1, CP-2A; opt CP-1D, CP-2D; forward/scenario (no block yet)".

Python computes a three-year annual path for two cases (base and downside): revenue, EBITDA, cash interest, free cash flow, debt and cash roll-forward, accessible liquidity, leverage, coverage, FCF to debt, the first year each CP-2A test is reached, and the leverage trajectory. The analyst chooses seven driver paths for the base case, the CP-2A scenario that defines the downside, the metric of each CP-2A watch level, a minimum liquidity (or "not assessed"), and the assessment, as literal submission input. No source states them, so they are labelled analyst assumptions (D11, as CP-2A Q1).

The r14 skill (`Deploy VI/reference/deploy-v-r14/skills/cp-2g-forward-credit-model/SKILL.md`, read as a reference, not in this repository) asks for base, upside and downside, quarterly detail when evidence supports it, a CP-MODEL driver table, named-driver scenarios, no plug, no zero substitution, no probability weighting. This version adopts: base and downside (the two cases the r14 semantic rules require and CP-MODEL consumes), annual granularity (the pack has one fiscal year), no plug (cash may go negative and is shown as an unfunded requirement), null never zero, no probabilities. Left out and recorded as limitations (full list and the FY2025 borrowing observation in `methods-cp2g-draft.md`): upside, quarterly path, finance-company perimeter split, new borrowing and refinancing, `cp2g.cp_model_forecast_drivers` (ruling 5).

```

  base_drivers: BaseDrivers                 # one entry per driver, no order, no extra key
  downside: Downside
  break_metrics: tuple[BreakMetric, ...]    # 0..8, one per CP-2A break whose origin is not "Definition"
  minimum_liquidity: MinimumLiquidity
  assessment: Assessment
  gaps: tuple[SynthesisGap, ...]            # 0..n, own type, as `cp2_synthesis.SynthesisGap` without `cp1a_refs` (cp2_synthesis.py:140; CP-2A lease :69); fields gap, why_it_matters, affected_analysis, severity, action
BaseDrivers: revenue_growth: GrowthDriver, ebitda_margin / capex_pct_revenue / tax_wc_pct_revenue / interest_rate: FractionDriver,
             dividends / debt_repayment: AmountDriver
XDriver:    values: tuple[str, str, str]   # FY2026, FY2027, FY2028, exactly three
            category: Category, rationale: str, limitation: str | None
Downside:   scenario: str (^s[0-9]{2}$, a CP-2A T2B.1 row id), rationale: str, limitation: str | None
BreakMetric: break_id: str (^b[0-9]{2}$), metric: Literal["total_leverage","net_leverage","interest_coverage"]
MinimumLiquidity: amount: str ("not assessed" or an amount), rationale: str
Assessment: rationale: str, category: Category, confidence: Confidence, limitation: str | None
```

Patterns: growth `^-?(0|1|0\.\d{1,4}|1\.0{1,4})$`; fraction (margin, capex, tax and working capital, rate) `^(0|1|0\.\d{1,4}|1\.0{1,4})$`; amount (distributions, repayment) `^[0-9]{1,6}(\.[0-9]{1,4})?$`; minimum liquidity amount the amount pattern or the literal `not assessed`. No computed number appears anywhere: prose uses `{ref|unit}` references only, to CP-1 facts (and CP-1D facts when bound) that carry a citation or a calculation. An uncited analyst cell (the CP-2A shock sizes and watch levels, this module's own driver cells) cannot be referenced in prose (`prose`: "Figure reference has no citation or calculation", probed), so the prose names the CP-2A scenario without a figure and the table shows the number. The schema has no digit rule for names, labels or prose; the literal prose of this oracle carries no digit outside a reference or a `CP-nn[A]` label only because the builder asserts it on its own literals (a digit written as a figure is the product's `Bare figure` refusal).

## Decimal composer: rules, zero / missing / not_applicable / blocked kept distinct

All amounts USD, unit `MILLIONS` (decimals 2, scale 1000000), multiples `MULTIPLE`, fractions `PERCENT`. Base period is fixed, not an analyst choice: the one CP-1 T4.10 column labelled `LTM Q4 25 (2025-01-01 to 2025-12-31)`; the forecast years are the three fiscal years after its end year (FY2026 to FY2028; year end 31 December from the identity source).

**Base operands** (`Operands.from_cp1`): exactly one T4.10 fact with label `LTM Q4 25 (…) · <metric>` for `revenue`, `ebitda`, `cash_interest`, `cash_taxes`, `working_capital_change`, `capex`, `fcf`, `total_debt`, `cash_closing`, `undrawn_rcf`, plus the reported ratios `total_leverage_reported`, `net_leverage_reported`, `fcf_to_debt` (reconciliation) and `ebitda_margin`, `capex_to_revenue` (prose). CP-1 stores the four outflows negative; the operand is the magnitude. A fact with status other than known makes the operand missing (never 0); a known 0 (`undrawn_rcf`) is a genuine zero.

**Engine** (R revenue, E EBITDA, I cash interest, X cash taxes plus working capital outflow, K capex, F free cash flow, D debt, C cash, U undrawn revolver; g, m, c, w, r, div, rep the drivers; s, k the CP-2A decline and increase; every quotient is one division of exact decimals, so the result is the correctly rounded rational):

| Quantity | Recipe |
|---|---|
| Revenue | `R(t) = R(t-1) x (1 + g)`, `R(0)` = CP-1 revenue |
| EBITDA | base `R x m`; downside `R x m x (1 - s)` |
| Cash interest | base `D(t-1) x r`; downside `D(t-1) x (r x (1 + k))` (opening balance, no circularity) |
| Cash taxes and working capital | `X = R x w`; capex `K = R x c` |
| Free cash flow | `F = E - I - X - K` (cash flow after interest, taxes and working capital is `E - I - X`) |
| Debt | `D(t) = D(t-1) - rep`; `D(0)` = CP-1 total debt; new borrowing is not modelled and has no column (ruling M4; limitation "New borrowing is not modelled" stays in the own limitations) |
| Cash | `C(t) = C(t-1) + F - div - rep`; `C(0)` = CP-1 cash; **not floored: negative cash is an unfunded requirement, never a plug** |
| Accessible liquidity | `C + U`, `U` = CP-1 undrawn revolver held flat |
| Ratios | total leverage `D(t)/E`, net leverage `(D(t) - C(t))/E` (iff `E > 0`); coverage `E/I` (iff `I > 0`); FCF to debt `F/D(t)` (iff `D(t) > 0`); a known 0 numerator gives a known 0.00x |
| Liquidity headroom | forecast years `Liq - minimum liquidity` (missing when not assessed); the FY2025 row `T2H.6.a25.liquidity_headroom` is `not_applicable` with the reason "Minimum liquidity is a forecast-year assumption and is not applied to the FY2025 actual" (ruling L2) |
| FY2025 row | recomputed from the CP-1 operands; `F` recomputed equals CP-1 `fcf`; leverage recomputed equals CP-1 reported (T2H.2 `check`) |
| Trajectory (T2H.8) | change = FY2028 total leverage - FY2025 total leverage; sign `<0` Deleveraging, `>0` Releveraging, `0` Flat; peak = highest of the three forecast years (missing if any year is not calculable), its period the peak year's FY period (the FY2026-FY2028 span when missing, ruling L1); `ebitda_fy26_vs_supported` = forecast FY2026 EBITDA - CP-1D supported EBITDA |

**Missing propagation.** A value is missing when any operand it needs is missing; its limitation is the root reason, first operand in formula order, then a non-positive denominator ("Not calculable: EBITDA is zero or negative (ZERO_OR_NEGATIVE_DENOMINATOR)"). Debt repaid to a known zero gives leverage 0.00x known, FCF to debt missing and, with the next year's interest 0, coverage missing (fixture `fixture_debt_repaid`). A missing CP-1 cash makes cash, liquidity, net debt and net leverage missing while gross leverage stays known (fixture `fixture_cash_missing`). A downside EBITDA of 0 gives leverage missing and coverage a known 0.00x (fixture `fixture_total_loss`).

**CP-2A consumption (D3).** The reader takes the downside scenario's `T2B.1.<row>.ebitda_decline` and `.interest_increase` and every row of the `T2B.6` register (`origin`, `test`, `level`, `required_decline`, `outcome`). The two definition breaks are fixed by CP-2A's wording: `b01` (test "Free cash flow at or below zero") is FCF at or below the level, `b02` ("EBITDA no longer covers cash interest") is coverage at or below the level; any other wording on an origin "Definition" row is a validator error. For every other row the metric is the analyst's `break_metrics` entry, **checked** against CP-2A's required decline: when CP-2A's base period equals this base period and the row has a known required decline, the CP-2A recipe (`s* = (L x E - D)/(L x E)` for total leverage, `(L x E - ND)/(L x E)` for net, `(E - L x I)/E` for coverage) applied to the CP-1 base must give exactly that value, else the issue `break_metrics[i]: metric <m> does not reproduce the CP-2A required decline of <row>`. When it cannot be checked the row is unverified and an own limitation line states the actual cause, one of three strings checked in this order: CP-2A stressed a different base period ("…so its required decline is not comparable"); CP-2A has no known level or required decline for it (missing or not applicable); a named CP-1 operand is missing (first in formula order, e.g. "base cash and cash equivalents is missing in CP-1 for LTM Q4 25: …"). Each reads "The metric named for CP-2A break <row> could not be checked against its CP-2A required decline: <cause>." (`fixture_cash_missing` b04 gives the CP-1 operand string, `fixture_cp2a_gap_head` b03 the base-period string; the second string has no fixture). Crossing is at or beyond the level (`<=` for FCF and coverage, `>=` for leverage). The tenth test `m01` "Accessible liquidity below the minimum liquidity" is the analyst's minimum liquidity, strictly below.

**First-break result** (T2H.7, per case): the first of the three years in which the test is met; if a year before it is not calculable the answer is `Not calculable`; none met and every year calculable is `Not reached by FY2028`. A `Not calculable` answer is not stored as a known text: the `base_first`/`down_first` fact has status `missing` with the root reason (the first year whose test is not calculable: the operand, the non-positive denominator, the CP-2A level, or "Not calculable: minimum liquidity was not assessed"), so it enters the gap register (ruling L5). The same treatment applies to `T2H.8.<case>.trajectory` (LEAD instruction, fix round 1): a trajectory that cannot be calculated is a `missing` fact with its root reason, with the own line "A leverage trajectory that cannot be calculated is missing, with the reason, not a known text."

## Refusals (validator issues; attempt 0 rejected, re-begun as attempt 1; two failures settle blocked)
