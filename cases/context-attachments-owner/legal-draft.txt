For the test date all debt is the sole senior secured term loan; no other debt, lease debt or debt adjustment exists.
Clause 2.2. Eligible Cash means the Group's reported cash and cash equivalents that are unrestricted and freely available to service Group debt. At December 31, 2025 all reported cash and cash equivalents are eligible, restricted cash is zero, and there are no short-term investments or other cash-like deductions. Covenant Net Debt means Covenant Debt less Eligible Cash; negative net debt, if any, is not floored at zero.
Clause 2.3. Covenant EBITDA means the Group's reported EBITDA: revenue less cost of sales less function-only operating expenses including depreciation plus depreciation and amortization, over the trailing twelve months ending on the test date. For December 31, 2025 the period is January 1 through December 31, 2025, identical to reported FY2025 and the four discrete calendar 2025 quarters. The underlying statements remain unaudited; this definition supplies no audit assurance.
