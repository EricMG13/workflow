### A-1 Conservation: an exact largest-remainder allocation. No tolerance.
**Defect (probed).** Two things break conservation today:
- Each share is rounded to 38 significant digits (cp4c.py:288).
- T4E.9 adds the shares in the rounding 38-digit context (`_RATIO`, :1915), then checks the balance (:1958).

The 38-digit sum can hide a residue. With c01 = 50 and three classes of 50 at rank 2, EV 150 gives an exact residue of −1E-36, yet T4E.9 shows 0. The report's case does raise: c01 = 50, three classes of 10, EV 55.62 gives a balance of 1E-36. So a balance of 0 does not prove conservation today. This is a failure-hiding rounding, on top of the residue itself.

**Rule (for `methods/cp4c.md` and as a lease amendment):**
1. Recovery stays `paid / group`, correctly rounded at 38 digits (unchanged).
2. When `paid = group`, each distribution is its claim, exactly.
3. When `0 < paid < group`, use the rank quantum `q = 10^(paid.adjusted() − 37)`, which is the 38th significant digit of `paid`. Compute each exact share `s_i = claim_i × paid / group` as a `Fraction`. Floor it to a multiple of `q`. Then hand the `(paid − Σ floors) / q` leftover units, one each, to the largest remainders. Ties go to the later class in the submission's claims order. Each share is within `q` of its exact value, is never negative, never exceeds its claim, and the rank's shares sum to `paid` exactly.
4. If `paid` or a claim in a partly paid rank is not a multiple of `q`, fail closed (new fail-closed state 14; it needs over 38 digits). (LEAD: `paid` added; without it the leftover unit count is not an integer.)
5. T4E.9 `creditor_distributions` is summed exactly: Inexact trapped, at a precision of at least 80, never `_RATIO`. It equals EV − residual. The balance is `ev − creditor_distributions − residual`, also exact, and must be 0. The :1958 raise becomes an invariant assert.
6. Shortfall stays `claim − distribution` rounded at 38 digits, as pinned. The oracle `T4E.4.v02c02.shortfall` = 16.253333333333333333333333333333333333; the exact value needs 39 digits. Record that per-class `claim = distribution + shortfall` is not an invariant. Only T4E.9 is.

**Worked example: 100 shared by 3 equal classes (claims 50 each).** `paid = 100`, so `q = 1E-35`. Each floor is 33.33333333333333333333333333333333333; the three floors sum to 100 − 1E-35, so one unit is left. The remainders tie, so it goes to the last class:
- c02 = c03 = 33.33333333333333333333333333333333333

## 1. Signs in figures read from quotes (a): read the sign

**Rule (one rule for every owner that reads a figure from an analyst quote; goes in each `methods/<cp>.md`).**
- A figure read from a quote is the signed numeral the quote prints. The sign is read on `quotes.normalise(quote)`, which maps U+2212 and the dashes to `-`.
- A `-` directly before the first digit is a minus sign when it starts the quote or follows a character that is neither a letter nor a digit. The pattern is `(?<![A-Za-z0-9])-`.
- So these are not signs: `1-December`, `2024-2025`, `95-96`, `S-350`.
- A `+` is ignored, because it does not change the value. Parentheses are not read as a sign in prose quotes (see For LEAD).

### Q-wrap-minus (CP-3D L1, CP-8 Info-1, CP-2E L1, CP-1D I1): rule W, "whitespace never separates a sign from its figure"

**Rule.** This amends round 10 §1's "directly before". It applies in the four quote readers only, on `quotes.normalise(text)` and on the joined reading, for both the quote and the page.
- **Minus.** A `-` is the figure's minus when it starts the text or follows a character that is neither a letter nor a digit, and the figure's first digit follows it either directly or after one space.
  - Pattern: `(?<![A-Za-z0-9])- ?`.
  - As a fixed-width exclusion for an unsigned token: `(?<![0-9.])(?<!(?<![A-Za-z0-9])-)(?<!(?<![A-Za-z0-9])- )<v>(?![0-9]|\.[0-9])`.
- **Accounting negative.** `(` or `( ` directly before the first digit, and `)` or ` )` directly after the last.
- **Why one space covers the whole class.** `normalise` turns any whitespace run into one space: a line break, CRLF, NBSP, a thin space, and `-\n` before U+2212. The joined reading differs from the plain one only by dropping that space after a wrapped `-`. So both readings now read the same sign at every figure, by construction.
- **What `-\n<digits>` is.** It is a minus after a space or at the start. After a letter or digit (`A-\n25`, `2024-\n2025`) it stays a hyphen wrap, which is not a sign.
