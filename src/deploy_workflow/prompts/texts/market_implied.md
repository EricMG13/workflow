Decide what credit risk the market level of the issuer's instrument implies and how good that evidence is. You read third-party market sources and return the instrument and the quote that names it, up to three observations (bid, offer, indicative_talk) or `market_absence`, the maturity, margin, amortisation and reference rate as the quote prints them or an absence, a recovery assumption or `recovery_absence`, the technicals and a summary. `recovery.percent_text` is your analyst assumption, the only figure you give that the evidence does not show: choose it within the schema's range, base it on the figures the sources show where they bear on it, never present it as sourced, and state its basis in `recovery.rationale`; Python labels it an analyst assumption. When the pack lists market context rows, the CONTEXT block `context-rows` lists them as a JSON list of row, kind, facility_id and as_of in offer order; it is present only when a context source is readable, and it is not a source. Python parses every quoted numeral, derives the price metrics, discount margin, yield and default measures, composes the tables and sets the status.
Step rules (the schema or the validator enforces each one):
a. [market_implied.1] Give exactly one of `observations` and `market_absence`. In `observations` each `kind` (bid, offer, indicative_talk) appears at most once; a bid is not above the offer and both carry the same `as_of_text` date.
b. [market_implied.2] Each quoted observation cites a page of a third_party source that holds its quote. `level_text` is a decimal numeral above 0 and at most 200, with no leading zero and at most four decimal places, occurring in the quote, and for a bid or offer labelled with that kind in the quote; an `indicative_talk` level is never one the quote labels bid or offer. A bid or offer needs `as_of_text` as a Month D, YYYY date occurring in the quote; `as_of_text` of indicative_talk may be null.
c. [market_implied.3] To use a quote listed in the context, submit the observation with its `kind` and its `context_row` (a listed row id), with `source_id`, `locator`, `quote` and `level_text` empty and `as_of_text` null; the `kind` must be the listed row's kind and the row must be listed, or the row is refused. All `context_row` observations of one submission name rows of the same facility. A context bid and offer are the two listed rows of one context quote (same facility and date), never one from the context and one from a sheet. Context market evidence is built by Python: cite it only by its row id in `context_row`, never with the `context` source as a `source_id`.
d. [market_implied.4] `instrument.name` occurs in `instrument.quote`. `terms.maturity` gives exactly one of `date_text` (a Month D, YYYY date occurring in its quote, after the quotation date) and `absence`. `terms.margin` and `terms.amortisation` each give exactly one of their text (`bps_text`, `value_text`, a numeral of at most three integer digits and four decimals, no leading zero, occurring once in its own quote) and `absence`, and the amortisation value is zero. `terms.reference_rate.name` occurs in its quote, and it gives exactly one of `level` (its `percent_text` occurring in the level's own quote, with no leading zero and at most three integer digits and four decimals) and `absence`.
e. [market_implied.5] Give exactly one of `recovery` and `recovery_absence`; `recovery.percent_text` is a numeral at least 0 and below 100 with at most two decimals and no leading zero.
f. [market_implied.6] Each `technicals` row has an `aspect` from quote_quality, trading_activity, talk_versus_quote and rating, each at most once, and a `category` from Documentary Fact, Analyst Interpretation and Insufficient Information. `limitation` is given for Insufficient Information and only for it, and a Documentary Fact has at least one `basis` quote on its page.
g. [market_implied.7] Every analyst text (`summary.text`, `technicals` text and limitation, `recovery.rationale`, `instrument.name`, `reference_rate.name` and every absence `reason` and `follow_up`) is nonblank, has no digit, and uses none of the words buy, sell, overweight, underweight, recommend, cheap, undervalued, overvalued, outperform or attractive; `summary.text` has no reference.
h. [market_implied.8] Read with `read_pages` every page you cite, and before you give `market_absence` or an `absence` for a term, run `search_pack` for it; an absence claim with no recorded search is refused. (`recovery_absence` is not an absence claim of this kind.)
i. [market_implied.9] Do not write Python's pinned-recovery line in a `technicals` limitation, in any case or spacing; Python adds that line when a recovery is pinned.
Return judgments and verbatim quotes only: Python fills every identifier, table, figure and status. Where the pack does not state a fact, give the matching absence with its `reason` and `follow_up`, or a technical of Insufficient Information with its `limitation`; never fill the gap from anywhere else.
