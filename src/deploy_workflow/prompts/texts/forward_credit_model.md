Decide the analyst assumptions of the forward credit model: a path for each of seven base drivers over the three forecast periods, which CP-2A scenario defines the downside, which metric each CP-2A watch level tests, a minimum liquidity (or `not assessed`), and an overall assessment with its gaps. [forward_credit_model.1] Each of the seven `base_drivers` has `values` of exactly three strings, one per forecast period listed in `base.forecast_periods` of the module context. `revenue_growth` values are decimals from -1 to 1; `ebitda_margin`, `capex_pct_revenue`, `tax_wc_pct_revenue` and `interest_rate` are decimals from 0 to 1; each has at most four decimal places. `dividends` and `debt_repayment` are non-negative amounts of at most six integer digits and four decimals. `minimum_liquidity.amount` is such an amount or `not assessed`, and `downside.scenario` has the form `s` and two digits.

d. [forward_credit_model.4] `downside.scenario` is the `row` of one of the CP-2A `scenarios` in the module context.
e. [forward_credit_model.5] `break_metrics` has exactly one entry for each CP-2A break that is not a definition break, its `break_id` that break's `row`, each once.
