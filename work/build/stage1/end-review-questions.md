# Stage 1 end review: questions for LEAD (one batch, sent after R1-R3)

- **R2 L1 (CP-L10 `change` exactness).** `cpl10.py:296-300` (`_q38`) stores `b - a` rounded to 38 digits HALF_EVEN with no Inexact trap, but lease :114 says "one exact subtraction". Two CP-1 38-digit ratios with different exponents need 39 digits (5/3 against 100/7: stored ...619047, exact ...6190473). The design note (stage1/cpl10/design-notes.md:9) chose 38 digits; no ruling amends the lease. Either amend the lease to 38 digits, or store exactly (or trap Inexact, as cp4c `_exactly` does). No frozen case is affected. Probe: stage1-end-R2/probes/p_r2_cpl10_change_exact.py (exit 1).
- **R1 M1 (CP-2D to CP-3C seam).** `cp2d.Cell` has no period, and `_gap_cell` drops `value_class` and `cp1_operand`. CP-3C needs a period on every known copied amount and a value_class on every copy (CP-3C lease :146, "the CP-2D head fact's own"); the CP-3C test fills both by hand. On `cp2d.compute` output, `cp3c.compute` raises on both positive and gap_minimum_cash. What period and value_class does CP-2D settle on each fact (proposed: the balance date for balances; a gap keeps its twin's class)? This needs a CP-2D oracle re-pin. Probe: stage1-end-R1/probes/p1_cp2d_to_cp3c_seam.py.
- **R1 M2 (CP-4C scenario lineage).** CP-4C lease :52/:69 picks the CP-2A T2B.1 row from the `cp2a:` lineage of T2H.4.d26.ebitda, but a settled Fact stores no lineage (`contracts.py:154-180`), and the cp2g_reader pin has none. How should CP-2D display a conflicting or not_applicable operand, and with what reason (cf. CP-1C 6g, CP-4C A-4)? Probe: p4_cp2d_status_collapse.py.
