Decide, for each rating agency in the module context, the direction of rating pressure: Downgrade pressure, Stable, Upgrade potential or Not assessable, with a confidence, a rationale and located citations from the agency source, then an overall assessment and the gaps. Python takes each rating and outlook from the context file's `ratings` rows when the context file holds `ratings` rows, otherwise from the agency source, and parses every trigger threshold from the agency source; it computes every headroom and timing figure, and composes the tables. The module context lists the register, the trigger terms, the forward ratios and the settled facts, and a rationale refers to a listed figure by reference only.
Step rules (the schema or the validator enforces each one):
a. [rating_triggers.1] Submit every field of the schema (`limitation` null when there is none) and no other, as valid JSON. `views` has at least one entry; each `view` is Downgrade pressure, Stable, Upgrade potential or Not assessable, `category` and `confidence` take only their listed values, and every view lists at least one `evidence` citation with its `source_id`, `locator` and `quote`.
b. [rating_triggers.2] Give exactly one view for each agency in `register.agencies` of the module context, its `agency` written exactly, and none for another agency or twice.
c. [rating_triggers.3] No view's `confidence`, and not `overall.confidence`, is High, because forward headroom rests on analyst-assumed drivers.
d. [rating_triggers.4] Every `rationale` and gap field is nonblank prose with no bare figure (digits only inside a period or filing label); a figure appears only as `{ref|unit}` with a `ref` and `unit` listed in the module context (`cp1_facts`, the `triggers` thresholds or the `cp2g` rows), closed and non-empty, and never a reference to a value that is not settled. A `limitation` is plain text: no figure and no reference. Write no peer ranking, instrument recommendation or recovery conclusion, no rating call (`will be downgraded`, `will be upgraded`, `will downgrade`, `will upgrade`, `is certain to be`, `our rating`, `we rate`, `shadow rating`, `implied rating`) and no probability weighting (`probability-weighted`, `expected value` and similar).
e. [rating_triggers.5] Cite each quote verbatim: a context row at its `line:N` in the `context` source, and an agency-source row under the locator of its page in the ratings source; a quote not at that locator is refused.
f. [rating_triggers.6] When the run context states a current period that is not a fiscal-year or fourth-quarter one, or one whose base year the CP-1 period register does not hold, or one whose register rows do not match the run (row currency, fiscal year, fourth-quarter end, year length or dates), or whose reporting currency is not the single currency the register reports, or whose fiscal year does not run 1 January to 31 December, or when that register is unusable, or when the current period is blank and its currency is not USD, or when the context file itself is refused, the submission is refused alone, before the rest of it is read; a different submission does not cure it, only a corrected context or pack.
Return judgments, references and verbatim quotes only: Python fills every identifier, rating, threshold, headroom and status. Where the evidence does not support a view, give Not assessable or state the `limitation` and the `gaps`; never complete the view from anywhere else.
