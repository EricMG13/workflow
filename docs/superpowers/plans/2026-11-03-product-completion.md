## Completion and cross-session contract

Product Completion is done when its required product work is reviewed/integrated and
documentation reflects Pre-Testing's acceptance. The project is complete only when
Pre-Testing's 26-owner/six-pathway proof is complete. Blocked validation returns to the
specific product task; it does not expand Pre-Testing into a general implementation lane.

The controlling end goal is accurate, fully populated deliverables, as defined in the
testing plan's "Definition of successful completion". Each module must contribute its
correct required information through genuine consumption into its assigned outputs;
each pathway must produce its complete, internally consistent deliverable set.
Pre-Testing freezes the artifact/section/content contracts and checks the visible
results against independent expectations. Implementation readiness or file generation
alone does not meet this end goal.

Both sessions may progress on disjoint work. Product changes never mutate an admitted
test checkout. Pre-Testing can prepare keys and fixtures while product work continues,
then freeze those instruments for each run. A new key or scorer version cannot
retroactively turn a product failure into a pass without its independent correction
record and the required re-evaluation.
